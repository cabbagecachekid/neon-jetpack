# Song Split Sheet

A split sheet records who wrote a song and each writer's ownership share. Fill it
out and sign it **at the time of writing**, before the song is released or earns
anything. It is not a government filing — it's the contemporaneous agreement that
prevents disputes and supports your registration and royalty claims.

> Informational template, not legal advice. For high-stakes or contested splits,
> have an IP/music lawyer review.

### Notes
- **Composition vs. recording are separate.** A co-writer of the song isn't
  automatically an owner of the recording, and vice versa. Use both tables when
  they differ.
- **Settle splits before release.** Renegotiating after a song does well is where
  friendships and money go to die.
- **Keep the signed copy** with your dated drafts and project files.
