# Filing prep — what to gather before you open eCO

Per-work-type checklists for US Copyright Office registration at **copyright.gov**.
Gather these *before* you start the online application so a filing session goes
fast. Informational only, not legal advice; the Office's exact fields and the
registration system change, so follow copyright.gov's current prompts.

General rule of thumb for every filing:
- **Claimant** (who owns it — usually you).
- **Author(s)** and what each authored.
- **Title.**
- **Year of creation**, and **date + nation of first publication** if published.
- **Deposit copy** of the work (the file you upload).
- **Eligibility for statutory damages** depends on timing — file **before
  infringement or within 3 months of first publication.**

---

## A solo song you wrote and recorded (human-made)

Two possible copyrights — decide what you're registering:
- **Composition (PA):** lyrics + melody. Deposit = lyric sheet and/or a recording.
- **Sound recording (SR):** your specific recording. Deposit = the audio file.
- If you own both and released them together, you may be able to register them
  on one application — follow the eCO prompts for "one work."

Gather: title, creation year, publication date/nation (if released), the audio
file and/or lyric sheet, your claimant info.

## A Suno / AI-assisted song

Register the **composition** and claim only the **human-authored** parts.
**File on the Standard Application, one song per filing** — works containing
AI-generated material **cannot be bundled into a group registration** (no GRUW/GRAM),
so each AI-assisted song is its own filing and its own fee.
Gather:
- Your **lyrics** (and notes on any melody **you** composed).
- A clear, written **description of what you did vs. what the AI generated** —
  reuse the AI section of `assets/split-sheet-template.md`.
- The disclaimer plan: in the application, **claim** human authorship (e.g.,
  "lyrics"), **exclude/disclaim** the AI-generated audio/arrangement in the
  limitation-of-claim fields, and add a brief note disclosing the AI use.
- **Do not** claim the AI-generated sound recording.
- If it's commercially important, route the claim past counsel before filing.

## Writing (lyrics as text, essays, articles, a manuscript)

Registered as a **literary work (TX)**.
Gather: title, author/claimant, creation year, publication details if published,
and the **text file** as the deposit. Unpublished pieces can be grouped — up to
**10 unpublished works** in one **GRUW** filing (one fee), but only if **every**
work shares the **same author/joint authors** and claimant, and none contain
AI-generated material (those need individual Standard Applications).

## Photographs

Registered as **visual arts (VA)**.
Gather: the **image files** (keep your highest-quality originals), titles or a
naming list, creation year(s), and publication info if published.
- **Group photographs:** up to **750** published *or* unpublished photos per
  filing — the efficient way to register a shoot or a backlog. Published and
  unpublished can't be mixed in the same group; published groups need all photos
  first published in the same calendar year.

## An album (multiple songs at once)

Use **GRAM** (Group Registration for Works on an Album of Music): up to **20
musical works** *or* **20 sound recordings** (plus associated liner notes/art)
on one album, created by the same author or sharing at least one common author,
in a single filing.
Gather: album title, track list with per-track authors and shares, creation and
release dates, and the deposit files. **AI-assisted tracks can't go in the GRAM
group** — pull them out and file each on its own Standard Application.

## A written-up UX framework / methodology

Register the **document** as a **literary work (TX)** (add **VA** for
standalone diagrams if needed). This protects your **text and figures from being
copied** — it does **not** protect the method itself.
Gather: the document file, title, creation year. Then decide separately whether
the *method* needs **patent**, the *name* needs **trademark**, or the know-how
should stay a **trade secret** — those are lawyer conversations, not eCO filings.

## After you file
- Save the **case/service request number** and the eventual **registration
  certificate** with your dated project files.
- Log the **registration date** — it's what determines your remedies.
- Set up royalty collection separately: **PRO** (ASCAP/BMI/SESAC/GMR) for
  performance, the **MLC** for streaming mechanicals. Registration protects;
  PRO/MLC collect.
